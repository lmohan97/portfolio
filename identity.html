Identity


This is my digital identity art. The base is a simple image of a school yearbook I found online, and edited using Photoshop. I was inspired by the 1990s and early 2000s aesthetic of doodles on yearbook images and scrapbook photos, along with a couple darker twists hinting at the tougher, more bleak side of growing up.
